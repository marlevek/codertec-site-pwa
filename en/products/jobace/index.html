JobAce: AI-powered job interview simulator

JobAce is an Android app with AI that analyzes your resume and conducts personalized interview simulations with real-time feedback. Available in Portuguese and English.

Visit JobAce

Who it's for

JobAce was designed for anyone who needs to prepare for job interviews with more confidence, method, and objective feedback.

Job seekers

Professionals who want to prepare for interviews with more confidence and receive objective feedback on their answers.

Students and recent graduates

Students who need to practice for their first selection processes without the pressure of a real interview.

Career changers

Those switching fields who need to adapt their communication and answers for a new job market.

What JobAce helps you solve

The app aims to eliminate insecurity, lack of practice, and absence of feedback in the job selection process.

Common challenges

  • Nervousness and insecurity in interviews
  • Not knowing how to present your experience
  • Lack of feedback on your answers
  • Difficulty with interviews in English
  • Generic answers that don't make you stand out

Key benefits

  • Realistic AI simulations that adapt to your profile
  • Detailed feedback after each answer (clarity, STAR method, content)
  • Personalized questions based on your resume
  • Practice in Portuguese and English in the same app
  • Progress report with score and improvement points
